Point Forecast is an Android client for U.S. National Weather Service point
forecasts. It is not affiliated with NOAA or NWS.

Features:

* Current conditions and multi-day NWS forecast
* Hourly tables (temperature, precipitation, wind, tides/water level,
  air quality, visibility, pressure, UV, space weather)
* Watches, warnings, and advisories
* Map location picker (osmdroid — no Google Play Services) and city search
* Sun, moon, space weather, earthquakes, and severe weather summaries
* Home screen widget with bundled NWS forecast icons

Privacy:

* No accounts, advertisements, or analytics
* Location is used only when you choose GPS or the map
* Preferences and cache stay on the device
* HTTPS only to public weather and map services
